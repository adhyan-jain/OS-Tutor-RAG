# Evaluation Blind Spots

For each metric family: what it can say, what it cannot measure, evidence, and whether the blind spot can become a rigorous research question. Literature evidence is from the 2026-09-30 area reports (partial reads; see `docs/LITERATURE_SEARCH_LOG.md`). "Project evidence" is from `FINDINGS.md` and `docs/CURRENT_PROJECT_AUDIT.md` (small-n, single-run, so only suggestive).

| # | Metric family | Can say | Cannot measure | Evidence | Rigorous question? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Faithfulness / entailment scores (RAGAS-style, NLI) | Whether answer text is supported by the provided context, on typical cases | Hard-case errors; factual or causal correctness of the context itself; abstention vs correctness | Ramprasad and Wallace 2024: drop on hard cases, gameable by appended sentences. Sufficient Context 2025: large models answer wrongly instead of abstaining. **Project evidence:** FINDINGS A1 (faithfulness rewards refusal) and A2 (nearly independent of correctness), n = 26 | **Yes.** Quantify the refusal/abstention confound and hard-case failure on a course corpus with expert labels |
| 2 | LLM-as-judge correctness | Broad preference agreement with humans for open-ended chat by a strong judge | Pedagogical correctness; factual/mechanistic correctness; same-family bias; order/length effects | MT-Bench (>80% agreement); order-swap flips; length bias; self-preference contested (Roytburg, Guey); MRBench kappa 0.65-0.71. **No judge-validity study on tutoring or OS answers found** | **Yes.** Teacher-labelled agreement with same- vs cross-family judges on OS explanations |
| 3 | Similarity to reference text (F1, cosine, BLEU/ROUGE) | Lexical/semantic overlap with a gold answer | Correctness when phrasing differs; correct answers with different framing; errors that keep vocabulary | KITE limitation; MisEdu-RAG's own best example contains a typo yet scores highest; word-overlap F1 "weak proxy" (2509.16780). **Project evidence:** A15 (`context_entity_recall` unreliable) | Yes, but weak novelty alone |
| 4 | Exact match / final-answer accuracy | Closed-form correctness | Valid reasoning; correct answers via shortcuts | Used by 81.3% of 445 benchmarks (Bean 2025); CES: 81% coherent traces but 47% correct, "suspiciously correct" outputs | Yes |
| 5 | Single-run scores without uncertainty | A point estimate | Whether a difference exceeds noise | Only 16.0% of benchmarks use uncertainty estimates (Bean); clustered SEs up to 3x naive (Miller); power ignored (Card 2020). **Project evidence:** A20 noise floor ~0.027 answer_correctness inverted the ranking of closely spaced retrieval arms | **Yes** as meta-research or as a protocol contribution; methodology is solved, adoption is the gap |
| 6 | Template / synthetic benchmarks with hard-coded scoring | Sensitivity to surface variation when anchored to a human-authored benchmark (GSM-Symbolic) | Representativeness; validity as a stand-alone measure | 47.5% of benchmarks use synthetic items (Bean). No paper validates stand-alone template scoring. **Project evidence:** OS-MechanismBench and its hard-coded 70/90 scoring (CURRENT_PROJECT_AUDIT A1-A2) | Only as a negative case; not a contribution |
| 7 | Unexecuted or assumed baselines | Nothing beyond the assumption | Any comparative claim | No paper directly studies this; reproducibility failures when setups differ (Biderman 2024). **Project evidence:** the "textual RAG accepts corrupted claim" arm is a literal `True` | Not a research question; a validity rule |
| 8 | LLM-simulated students | Item-difficulty ordering with ensembles (Liu, abstract only) | Realistic misconceptions/dialogue behavior; transfer to real learners | Benedetto r = 0.13; Yuan "competence paradox"; PedagogicalRL not validated with real students | **Yes** if real distractor-choice data exist |
| 9 | Tutor-quality benchmarks (MRBench, MathTutorBench) | Turn-level pedagogical quality as rated | Effect on learning; safety | Authors' own limitations; no link from score to learning gain found | Yes but needs learners |
| 10 | Immediate post-test after AI session | Short-term performance | Retention, transfer, dependency | Kestin (immediate); Bastani (withdrawal test showed harm, abstract only); dissociation RCT (scores up, learning not) | Yes but needs cohort |
| 11 | Perceived helpfulness / satisfaction | User perception | Learning | CodeHelp, CodeAid (no outcomes); Guardrails RCT: favorability != effectiveness; ai2503: students cannot tell LLM MCQs apart yet score ~9% lower | Yes |
| 12 | Explanation *verified* by a tool | Claims that the tool can check, locally | Claims outside tool coverage; global correctness | FAX: "verified" is local; MechSim reports LLM-judge scores, not claim-level accuracy; **claim coverage rarely reported** | **Yes.** Claim-coverage and claim-level false-accept/reject against expert labels |
| 13 | Correct forward simulation | What happened | Why it happened (causal attribution) | TempoBench: 75-96% simulation vs 21-33% minimal causal attribution | Yes; extendable to OS traces |
| 14 | Code-execution benchmarks | Python algorithmic execution | OS-level state (scheduler, paging, locks); concurrency | Demystify, CodeCrash, NExT state Python-only limitations | Yes |
| 15 | Contamination checks | Detected overlap under stated assumptions | Effect on judges/verifiers; contamination of small course corpora | 2410.18966 (assumptions may not hold); unsearched for course material | Weak; low priority |
| 16 | Near-duplicate documents in corpus | (nothing) | Retrieval bias when the same lecture exists in multiple versions/formats | **Project evidence only:** the merged corpus has PDF+PPTX pairs and two versions of several lectures. No literature searched (UNKNOWN) | Possible, unsearched; treat as a data-quality control, not a contribution |

## Notes on the project's own evidence
Blind spots 1, 3 and 5 are the ones the project has *real measurements* on (2026-07 runs, `eval/pipeline_runs.xlsx`, A20 confirmed against the workbook). They are small (26 questions, single run) and the corpus has since grown from 11 to 37 files, so any claim must be re-measured on the new corpus with a new, larger and independently reviewed question set.
