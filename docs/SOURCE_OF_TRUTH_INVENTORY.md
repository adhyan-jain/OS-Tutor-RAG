This resolves the earlier "11 files" coverage limit; the "22-document corpus" wording in FINDINGS.md remains unreconciled.
- Some lectures now exist in more than one version or format (for example `Introduction to OS.pptx` with 10 slides vs `1. Introduction to OS.pptx` with 20 slides; `paging (1).pptx` vs `15. Paging.pptx`; PDF+PPTX pairs). Treat near-duplicates as a data-quality control.
- The legacy `.doc` exercise sheet is skipped by the ingestion code (its `.docx` twin is indexed).
- Backups of the previous `data/raw`, `data/index` and `data/processed` are in `data/_backup_20260930/`.
- **All July evaluation runs used the 11-file corpus and the old index, so they are not comparable with anything measured on this corpus.**
